# Book2Trek policies & FAQ (RAG knowledge base)

## Cancellation policy
Bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to 7 days before the trek start
date. Cancellations within 7 days are eligible for a 50% refund. No-shows are
non-refundable. To cancel, use "cancel my booking" in the app or ask the voice
assistant.

## Safety and fitness
Every trek lists a difficulty (Easy, Moderate, Hard). Hard treks require prior
high-altitude experience and a basic fitness check. Guides carry first-aid kits
and satellite communicators on all multi-day treks.

## What to pack
Layered clothing, broken-in trekking shoes, a 2-litre water capacity, sunscreen,
and a headlamp. Multi-day treks include tents and meals; day treks do not.

## Slots and group size
Each trek has a fixed number of slots. When available slots reach zero the trek
is fully booked. Group sizes are capped so guides can maintain a safe ratio.

## Payments and refunds
Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days
after an eligible cancellation is approved.

## Rescheduling
A booking can be moved to another open date for the same trek at no cost, subject
to availability, up to 3 days before departure.
